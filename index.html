About Our Church

A community rooted in faith, love, and service

Grace Community Church has been a spiritual home for families in our city for over 30 years. We are a welcoming congregation dedicated to worship, fellowship, and serving our neighbors with the love of Christ.


Whether you're exploring faith for the first time, returning after time away, or looking for a church family to call home — you are welcome here. Come as you are.
